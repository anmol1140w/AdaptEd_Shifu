# Datasets

Generated, machine-ingestible data belongs here. Human-authored curriculum content remains in the sibling `books/`, `concepts/`, `lessons/`, `items/`, `references/`, `seeds/`, and `scenarios/` areas.

- [`ncert/`](ncert/README.md): extracted NCERT PCM text prepared as LangChain-compatible JSONL corpora for retrieval and vectorization experiments.

Source PDFs and their book-level bibliographic records stay under [`../books/ncert/`](../books/ncert/README.md). Keeping source material separate from derived datasets makes provenance and rebuilds clear.
