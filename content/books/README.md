# Books

One JSON book record per subject and class. Files follow the `subject_class_number.json` pattern, such as `physics_class_11.json`. Each is the table of contents and stable mapping from chapter/topic to concept, lesson, and chunk IDs. Authored instruction lives in `lessons/`; generated retrieval corpora live in `datasets/`; source bibliographic records live in `references/`.

Official NCERT English source books for Classes 11 and 12 are under [`ncert/`](ncert/README.md). Their per-class/subject parts, chapter PDFs, archive URLs, and extraction details are indexed in [`ncert/catalog.json`](ncert/catalog.json). Class 11 Mathematics is a single-volume NCERT book in the selected catalog, rather than two separately published parts.
