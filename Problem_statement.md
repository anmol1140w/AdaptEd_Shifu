PS-22 — AI-Powered Adaptive Learning & Skill Gap Identification Platform Generative AI + Education Technology Problem Background Most educational institutions follow a standard teaching methodology despite students having different learning speeds, capabilities and levels of understanding. Existing assessments primarily measure marks rather than identifying specific learning gaps and recommending personalised improvement strategies. The Challenge Develop an AI-powered adaptive learning platform capable of analysing student performance, identifying skill gaps and recommending personalised learning paths. Expected Features ● Conduct adaptive assessments based on a student’s performance. ● Identify subject-wise and concept-wise knowledge gaps. ● Generate personalised learning recommendations using AI. ● Provide an AI-powered learning assistant capable of explaining difficult concepts. ● Generate quizzes based on a student’s weak areas. ● Develop a teacher dashboard showing student performance, learning progress and intervention recommendations. Expected Deliverable A functional learning platform demonstrating student assessments, AI-generated learning recommendations and teacher analytics. Suggested Technologies: Python, React, FastAPI, LLM APIs, RAG, PostgreSQL, Machine Learning Difficulty: Intermediate to Advanced SDG Alignment: SDG 4 – Quality Education
